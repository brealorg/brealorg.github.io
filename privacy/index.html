Privacy policy

Effective date: 30 July 2026.

This policy describes the subshell Android application and the public website at brealorg.github.io. subshell is an independent client project and is not affiliated with Reddit.

Data processed by the Android app

When Reddit account access is enabled and you choose to connect an account, authentication occurs through Reddit. OAuth credentials are stored in app-private encrypted storage backed by Android Keystore. Authentication credentials are not included in subshell settings backups.

The app may process account identifiers, Reddit listings, posts, comments, subscriptions, and actions you explicitly request. Those requests are exchanged directly between the app and Reddit.

For offline operation, the app may store Reddit content and account metadata in its private local Room database. App settings and settings history are stored locally. Settings backups are written only to a folder selected by the user. Downloaded media remains on the device until the user removes it.

Data not collected by the project

subshell does not currently operate a project backend, analytics service, advertising system, or telemetry service. The project does not sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising.

This website

This static website uses no JavaScript, analytics, forms, advertising, or cookies. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security purposes under GitHub's own policies.

Third-party services

Reddit processes information when the app connects to Reddit services. GitHub processes information required to host this website. Their own terms and privacy policies govern their processing.

Retention and deletion

Logging out removes the corresponding local authentication session. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app removes app-private local data. User-selected settings backups and downloaded media are separate files and must be deleted from their storage locations by the user.

Contact and changes

Privacy and support requests can be made through the support page. This policy will be updated when the application's data flows materially change.
